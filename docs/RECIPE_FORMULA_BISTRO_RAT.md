## Rat Level puzzle and state conditions

1. Reveal the anti-gravity control by interacting with the picture / portrait.
2. Turn on anti-gravity; floating objects expose a note in the planter.
3. Click the wine bottle label to expose the keypad.
4. Enter the three-digit code from the planter note.
5. Unlock the cooker power control and test both power levels.
6. Do not assume OFF = low or ON = high until verified on the device.
7. Coioioin: click the coin beside the cooker until the same face lands three times consecutively, matching the official Steam achievement text and multiple guides. A separate blue-switch / red-target report is uncorroborated and is not a required prerequisite in this spec.

The numeric code itself is not present in the readable sources. Do not hardcode a guessed number.

## Bistro dependency and interaction conditions

- Mixer must be repaired / powered via the planter electrical panel; a switch is hidden beneath plant leaves.
- Scissors are obtained by placing the empty blender jar in the pot; scissors are then used to reveal the hidden switch and cut the hanging ingredient rope.
- Rat trade begins by offering Cheese at the extractor-hood door. The rat displays a dish drawing; making it trades for an ingredient. First player-recorded reward: Tomato. Later rewards include Apple, Olive, Garlic.
- Seven country dishes create seven magnets. Only Mochi/Japan is explicitly named in readable text; do not infer the other six.
- Five wine bottles are ordered by label height, shortest to tallest. Actual bottle identities / order need screenshot verification.
- Mixer outputs are separate states: Ground Meat, Ground Tomato, Ground Pumpkin, Ground Apple, Ground Rice, Minced Garlic, Shredded Cheese.
- 1.0 candidate total is 84 Good / 39 Bad. The 7/14 guide lists 76/31; July 17 player comments add Crazy Calzone (Good) and five Bad recipes; 8/27 adds 7 Good + 2 Bad; 1.0 completion report adds Clutter. This is a cross-source candidate count, not an official cookbook counter.

## Rat Level — 2026-11-17 source reconciliation (SRC-047)

The directly readable 9Puz 1.0 route independently confirms the puzzle sequence and clarifies the slot sweep:
- Reveal the anti-gravity control from the upper-right mouse portrait; activate it; read the three-digit code on the planter note; peel/click the wine-bottle label to expose the keypad; enter the note's code.
- Cold Good candidate order: Cheese; Cheese + Cheese; Cheese + Paper; Cheese + Pencil; Cheese + Eggshell.
- Hot Good candidate order: Cheese; Cheese + Cheese; Cheese + Paper; Cheese + Pencil.
- Rat cooker input limit: two items per attempt.
- Bad candidates include empty cooker, Leaf, Paperclip + Paper, Egg + Pencil, Leaf + Pencil, Paperclip + Pasta, Eggshell + Paper, and single Paperclip / Paper / Eggshell / Pencil plus Pencil + Paper. Preserve source conflicts for Egg vs Eggshell + Pencil and Pasta-related slots; do not flatten into one formula.
- 9Puz confirms the three-digit value itself is read from the in-game planter note; it does not transcribe the digits. Numeric code remains UNKNOWN.
- 9Puz calls the two cooker states cold/hot; the Japanese 1.0 walkthrough calls them weak/strong power. Keep oven_state and power_level as separate source-facing fields until an image or in-game test establishes the mapping.
- 9Puz's Bistro route says Big Magnet is made by cooking a rat-reward Magnet; Compass is Scissors + a country Magnet only after the magnet has registered for Going Global.

Evidence: SRC-047 (directly readable 1.0 community walkthrough). These are candidate rules, not in-game verification.
