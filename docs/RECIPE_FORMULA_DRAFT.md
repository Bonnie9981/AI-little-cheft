## Modeling rules

- Every ingredient and processed state is a separate item or item-state record.
- Appliance is part of the recipe condition: Cooking Pot, Microwave Oven, Espresso Machine, and Rat Level Electric Can Cooker are not interchangeable.
- Cafe coffee states must remain distinct: Coffee Beans, Espresso Shot, Filled Coffee Cup, Cup of Espresso, Double Espresso.
- Student Dorm toast states must remain distinct: Bread, Toast, Burnt Toast, Soggy Toast.
- A formula marked alternate or conflicting is not a definitive alternate recipe until verified in-game.
- Do not treat “any unrelated ingredient” recipes as a fixed exact list; model them as category predicates only if in-game behavior confirms the predicate.
